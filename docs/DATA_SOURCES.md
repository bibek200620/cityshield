# Data sources and provenance

The verified local runs use one 2025-04-14 Bellandur Landsat 8 acquisition and 20 Landsat 8/9 city acquisitions documented in [the heat workflow](HEAT_WORKFLOW.md) and [Bengaluru evaluation](BENGALURU_EVALUATION.md). Scene and model artifacts are kept in ignored local storage. The checkout now includes a checksum-verified 53-row, district-scale historical event subset from IFI-Impacts v3; it is not used for flood training or model evaluation. See [flood event provenance](FLOOD_EVENT_DATA.md). The route weather view requests current plus three future hourly estimates. The tools-menu report requests the next 48 hourly forecast estimates from Open-Meteo when the provider is reachable.

### India administrative outlines

- State outlines use the [geoBoundaries India ADM1 GeoJSON](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM1/), sourced from [DataMeet](https://projects.datameet.org/maps/states/) and licensed CC BY 2.5 India. Its metadata declares a 2011 boundary vintage; use these as visual orientation only, not legal boundaries.
- District outlines use the [geoBoundaries India ADM2 GeoJSON](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/IND/ADM2/), licensed ODbL 1.0 and representing 2021. Coordinates are rounded to 0.001 degrees for a lighter map asset.
- These layers are limited to India and are loaded at different zoom levels. District boundaries can help identify places but are not municipal city limits; complete, current municipal boundaries for every Indian city are not present in this dataset.

### Bengaluru local model readiness

- The checked-in Bengaluru research artifact predicts next-local-day maximum 2 m air temperature from the previous local day’s complete hourly temperature and humidity summaries. It was trained on 2000–2020 reanalysis data and evaluated on 2021–2025 reanalysis-derived targets (MAE 0.78 °C; 1,826 dates); this is not station-observation validation or an operational forecast.
- The live app does not retain a complete prior local day of weather inputs. This research model is therefore not wired into the current forecast UI. The city-scene heat model predicts same-acquisition satellite land-surface temperature; it is not a weather forecast model.

## Validation data gaps

- No flood incident records are used for model training or event-level evaluation. The available IFI-Impacts subset is date/date-range and district-scale; it has no localized outcome geometry or matched rainfall.
- A separate offline GHCNh station-observation evaluation exists for one airport site; it is discontinuous and the candidate model did not beat persistence. No station-validated, citywide heatwave forecast is configured. The ERA5-Land experiment remains model-derived, and the short-range Open-Meteo outlook is a separate forecast-model feed.
- The Bellandur metrics apply to a spatial holdout within one acquisition. The city study reports a separate buffered spatial-block holdout across training acquisitions and a later-date holdout. A fresh checkout requires local scene/model files for Bellandur inference; city study scene inputs can be retrieved from the documented Planetary Computer collection when network access is available.
- The synthetic grid is positioned inside the Bellandur bbox for map comparison; this does not make its terrain, roads, drainage, or facilities local observations.
